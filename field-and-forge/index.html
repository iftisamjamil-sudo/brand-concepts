01

Cleansing Facial Oil

$42

A gentle yet powerful cleansing oil that melts away sunscreen, makeup, and impurities while nourishing the skin barrier. Crafted with a blend of plant-based oils rich in antioxidants and essential fatty acids, this formula leaves skin balanced, soft, and radiant.

It dissolves makeup, sunscreen, and daily buildup, restores hydration, and is gentle enough for sensitive skin.

Ingredients
Squalane, rosehip oil, red raspberry seed oil, abyssinian oil, vitamin E, blue tansy oil.
How to use
Massage 2–3 drops into dry skin using hands or gua sha. Remove with a warm washcloth. For extra hydration, reapply 1–2 drops and pat into skin.

02

Daily Mineral Defense

$26 1 oz

Clean, mineral-first skincare built for surf, snow, and sunny trail days. This balm-like formula blends non-nano zinc oxide with nourishing botanicals to protect, tint, and hydrate in one smooth step.

Non-nano zinc shields skin without chemicals. Natural cacao warms and evens skin tone. Mango butter, jojoba, and castor oil cushion, soothe, and protect. Sweat-resistant, reef-safe, and pregnancy-friendly.

Ingredients
Mango butter, jojoba oil, castor oil, non-nano zinc oxide, vitamin E (tocopherol), cacao powder.
How to use
Apply a small amount to skin and blend evenly. Reapply as needed.
Sizes
Full size 1 oz (30 g), $26. Mini round .5 oz (15 g), $14.

The founder

Karissa Campbell

A former platform diver turned surfer, snowboarder, and mountain biker, Karissa created Field + Forge to make simple, plant-based, acne-safe skincare that protects the skin barrier and performs in real life.

After years of struggling with acne and inflammation, she began formulating her own gentle, effective blends to calm, restore, and strengthen her skin. What started as a personal ritual became Field + Forge: small-batch essentials that favor quality over clutter.

Rooted in outdoor life and crafted in Bend, Oregon, every jar is intentionally made to be non-toxic, non-comedogenic, and barrier-supportive — no unnecessary fragrance or fillers.

She believes skincare should feel grounded, calming, and easy to use — every day.

Journal · October 25, 2025

Why the ingredient lists stay this short.

When we say functional skincare, born from the elements, we mean it. Every oil, butter, and mineral we use is chosen for its proven ability to protect, nourish, and restore — without the unnecessary fillers or harsh synthetics that can disrupt your skin barrier.

We believe your skin thrives on consistency and simplicity. That’s why every Field + Forge formula is intentionally minimal — no synthetic fragrances, no unnecessary fillers, no complicated steps.

Small-batch skincare crafted in Oregon. Because taking care of your skin should feel natural — never complicated.
