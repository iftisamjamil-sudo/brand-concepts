About the name

Hope, hand-poured.

We’re Andy and Selvia, the husband and wife behind Wick of Hope. We’ve been together since high school. We got married young, and somewhere along the way decided we wanted to build something of our own.

What pushed us forward wasn’t ambition. It was everything we were going through privately. Around the time we were dreaming up this company, we lost two pregnancies. Our first miscarriage was the hardest. We saw the heartbeat, and a short while later we were told the news no parent ever wants to hear.

We didn’t talk about it much back then. Most people who lose a pregnancy don’t. But it changed us, and it changed what we wanted this brand to be.

We named it Wick of Hope because hope is a word everyone carries differently.

The wick is the struggle. The flame is the hope.

Small, steady, sometimes the only light in the room.

Est. January 2024

We started small, really small. Just the two of us in our basement, surrounded by wax, wicks, and the lingering smell of trial and error. We didn’t have fancy equipment or a team. After work, we’d head downstairs and keep pouring, refining every detail, from the crackle of the wooden wicks to the way each scent filled a room.

What started with Andy’s sensitivity to paraffin wax and overpowering fragrances became a mission to craft cleaner candles, made for mindful homes. We’re proudly Ontario Made, proudly small, and proudly first-generation Canadians who started this with no investors and no plan to make it anything but a family business.

From the basement to the warehouse, some things have changed, but what matters never will. We still hand-pour every candle with the same care as day one. What fills your home should never harm it.

How a candle is made here

Made with care

Hand-poured in small batches in London, Ontario, and checked by Andy and Selvia before it ships. Coconut soy wax. An FSC-certified wooden wick with a soft crackle. Biodegradable packaging.

Made without

No paraffin, phthalates, parabens, or dyes. Essential oils or clean fragrance oils, chosen so the scent stays balanced rather than overpowering. Vegan. Cruelty-free.

Made to give back

Through i=Change, every order contributes to a cause chosen at checkout: planting trees, supporting women’s health, lifting communities out of poverty. Through Greenspark, every candle helps fund ocean plastic recovery, kelp forest restoration, and reforestation.
